Greetings, program!

We are building a website for an emerging community of people trying to care for each other during the unfolding UFO disclosure moment. It's currently just an informal group of folks, but we need a bit of structure so we can make time to be together to talk about what's going on and make sure all of the folks in our local community are cared for in the process. We are located in Columbus, Ohio, but we might provide subdomains for other groups in other locations.

I want you to help me build and maintain a jekyll website hosted on github pages. We want it to be lightweight and easy for now. We can always upgrade to something more substantial later. I think a few pages, a blog, an events calendar. You and I will be the ones maintaining the site.

If you read over those sites, you should have a pretty good idea of what we are up to. And I've given you the transcripts of my lectures. That should help too. 

We might want to utilize some historical ufo photos, but we'd have to think about which ones to use. I've given you my favorite one in the images directory. Definitely use that one.

Feel free to throw around some of the standard alien emoji to spice things up and make them feel cute and welcoming. But don't go crazy. We want the site to be fun and welcoming.

If you read over the Visible College content, you will get a good idea of the epistemic vibe we want to go for. Open minded and curious and welcoming and non-dogmatic. This is a puzzling time and we need to have grace for each other as we try to figure out what it all means. We need to honor and respect existing philosophical and religious traditions while recognizing that all of us will need to stretch a bit. And no doubt we will have to revisit old questions, like how indigenous communities were treated. There's an open letter on the visible college page that you should read too.
