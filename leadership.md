The Disclosure Community is intentionally informal. We do not have
officers or a board. Our co-leads &mdash; [Grant Eckhart](#grant-eckhart),
[Patrick Dunn](#patrick-dunn), and [Steven Brown](#steven-brown) &mdash;
help convene these conversations and keep the gatherings going.
[Marti Houghton](#marti-houghton) coordinates our events, and
[Adam Messmer](#adam-messmer) handles communications.
