The Disclosure Community is intentionally informal. We do not have
officers or a board. We do have three friends who took the initiative
to start convening these conversations in Columbus, and who help keep
the gatherings going: [Grant Ekhart](#grant-ekhart),
[Patrick Dunn](#patrick-dunn), and [Steven Brown](#steven-brown).

---

## Grant Ekhart {#grant-ekhart}

**Pastor · Author**

Pastor at [Jacob's Porch](https://www.jacobsporch.com/), the Lutheran
student ministry at The Ohio State University. Grant writes
[Ministry in the Weird](https://ministryintheweird.substack.com/),
thinking through how the church meets a world that is stranger than
its old categories.

---

## Patrick Dunn {#patrick-dunn}

**Priest · Author**

Priest at [Mud Lotus Sangha](https://www.mudlotussangha.org/).
Patrick writes [Dharma Between Worlds](https://dharmabetweenworlds.substack.com/),
a quiet, careful Buddhist engagement with disclosure, contact, and
what these phenomena ask of practice.

---

## Steven Brown {#steven-brown}

**Professor · Lecturer**

Professor of philosophy at [The Ohio State University](https://philosophy.osu.edu/people/brown.2703),
founder of [the Visible College](https://thevisiblecollege.org/),
and a regular lecturer on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@stevenbrownphilosophy)
on disclosure, the paranormal, and what philosophy has to say about
any of it. More at [stevenbrownphilosophy.com](https://stevenbrownphilosophy.com/).
