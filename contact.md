The simplest way to reach us is by email:

Tell us a little about yourself, what brought you here, and whether
you&rsquo;d like to be looped in on upcoming gatherings. We try to write
back within a week or so &mdash; sometimes faster, sometimes slower,
depending on what life is up to.

## In person

We meet in and around Columbus, Ohio. Locations vary by gathering:
sometimes a campus space, sometimes a back room at a coffee shop,
sometimes someone&rsquo;s living room. Specifics go out by email a few
days before each event.

## Online

If you&rsquo;re outside central Ohio and want to connect, drop us a
line anyway. We are slowly figuring out how to fold remote folks into
some sessions, and we are open to helping people in other cities start
something similar in their own neighborhoods.

## A small ask

If you&rsquo;re reaching out because you&rsquo;ve had an experience
you&rsquo;re trying to make sense of, please know: you are welcome,
and we will take you seriously. There is no test to pass. We are not
here to debunk you or to canonize you. We just want to listen.
