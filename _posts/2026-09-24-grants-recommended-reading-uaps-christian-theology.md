This is my personal recommended reading list. It brings together foundational UAP research, experiencer testimony, selected consciousness and speculative material, biblical and Second Temple texts, and the theological works I have found most helpful for thinking across these subjects.

## Foundational UAP Research

- **Jacques Vallée** — *Passport to Magonia: From Folklore to Flying Saucers*
- **D. W. Pasulka** — *American Cosmic: UFOs, Religion, Technology*
- **James T. Lacatski, Colm A. Kelleher, and George Knapp** — *Skinwalkers at the Pentagon: An Insiders’ Account of the Secret Government UFO Program*
- **David M. Jacobs** — *Secret Life: Firsthand Accounts of UFO Abductions*
- **John E. Mack** — *Abduction: Human Encounters with Aliens*
- **J. Allen Hynek** — *The UFO Experience: A Scientific Inquiry*
- **Patrick Jackson** — *The Sphere Network*
- **Beatriz Villarroel and collaborators** — VASCO research and related studies of anomalous and transient sources in Palomar Observatory Sky Survey I (POSS-I) plates
